## <a id="section-2"></a>2. What data we collect

**Account.** Your name, which we store as a first and last name, your email address (confirmed with a one-time code before the account is created), and, if you add it, your phone number.[ With Telegram sign-in: An account created through Telegram gets a placeholder email address instead, which we never send email to, until you change it to a real one.]

**Sign-in.** We use passkeys instead of passwords[, and you can also sign in through our mini app in Telegram]. We store your passkey's public key and identifier, never your fingerprint, face or device PIN, which stay on your device. For each device you sign in on we store an identifier the app creates when it is installed, the device name the system reports (usually its model), sign-in tokens, and when you last signed in. We record the date you were last active so that we can delete unused accounts ([section 7](#section-7)).

**Notifications.** Your device's push notification token and language, your notification settings, and whether you turned on email[ or Telegram] notifications.

[If you turn on Telegram sign-in or notifications: **Telegram, only if you use {{SERVICE_NAME}} in Telegram.** When you sign in through our mini app in Telegram, Telegram sends us, signed by Telegram, your Telegram user ID, your first and last name and, if you have one, your username. We create your account from that name. Telegram does not share your email address or phone number with us. We store your Telegram user ID and username, and update the username every time you sign in through Telegram. Our staff see your Telegram username on your client record, so they can reach you. If Telegram notifications are on, our bot sends your notifications to your private chat with it. When you ask in the mini app to delete your account or export your data, our bot sends you a message to confirm it; its button opens the mini app, which sends us a confirmation signed by Telegram. The mini app also uses your Telegram language and, if your Telegram privacy settings allow it, shows your Telegram profile photo; it loads both on your device from Telegram and doesn't send them to us. We use nothing else from Telegram, such as your contacts or chats.]

**Legal document records.** Which versions of this policy you acknowledged and of the [Terms of Service]({{TERMS_URL}}) you accepted, and when.

**Support.** If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, the contents of your message and anything you attach, such as usage logs.

**Your client record and appointments.** Your name, phone and email, appointments and appointment requests (services, employee, time, price, status, the note you write, cancellation and decline reasons, completion details and price adjustments with their reasons), and any description our staff add to your client record. When you change your name, phone or email in your profile, your client record and employee profile are updated to match; if we already had another client record with your new confirmed email, it is merged into yours.

**Sources.** We receive information from you, our staff who create client or employee records, the person booking for you, and selected device contacts our staff lawfully import. We provide privacy information to people who do not have an account through {{INDIRECT_NOTICE_METHOD}}, within the applicable legal deadlines.

**If you work for us.** Your employee profile (name, contact details, services, working hours, days off, permissions, and whether your access is suspended) and the appointments assigned to you. While your access is not suspended, anyone signed in to {{SERVICE_NAME}} can see your name, services, working hours, days off and the times you are free, so they can book you.

**Data exports** you request, kept until they expire ([section 7](#section-7)).

**Contacts, only if our staff import clients.** Our staff can import clients from their phone's contacts after allowing the app to access them. The app reads the contacts on the device and shows them; only the contacts selected (name, phone and email) are sent to our server and become client records. The address book itself is never uploaded.

[If your app build includes Firebase Crashlytics, as the BookkMe client does: **Crash reports.** When the app crashes, it sends Firebase Crashlytics technical details about the error and your device (model, operating system and app version, an installation identifier, and the time of the crash), so we can fix it. We do not deliberately attach your account identifier, name, email or business/client records. Crash traces can include exception messages, so reports are not guaranteed to be anonymous. Crash reports are off by default and are sent only if you turn them on in Settings → Account → Privacy. You can turn them off there at any time, which also deletes reports not yet sent.]

**On your device.** The app keeps a copy of the data you see on your device so it opens quickly. The copy is deleted when you sign out.[ With Telegram: In our mini app in Telegram, the copy, your sign-in tokens and your language choice are kept in the storage Telegram's in-app browser gives our web app; there is no sign-out there, so they stay until you clear that browser data in Telegram.] If you turn on biometric unlock, the check happens on your device and we receive nothing.

**Technical data.** Your IP address and request details pass through our servers to route requests and protect the service against abuse. Our server logs may contain your IP address, account identifiers and error details.

We do not access your location or photos through {{SERVICE_NAME}}, the app uses no analytics or advertising identifiers, our emails do not track whether you open them or click their links, we do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell it.

## <a id="section-4"></a>4. Notes and sensitive information

## <a id="section-7"></a>7. How long we keep it

|---|---|

An account not used for 3 years is deleted; we warn you 60, 30 and 7 days before. An owner's account is kept while another member of the business has been active within that time. |
| A device you signed in on | Deleted after 30 days without use. Its push notification token is removed when you sign out. |
| [With Telegram] Your Telegram user ID and username | Until your account is deleted. A changed or removed username replaces the old one the next time you sign in through Telegram. |
| Email confirmation codes | 10 minutes. |
| Your client record | Until {{CLIENT_RETENTION_MONTHS}} months after your last visit or the last change to it, then deleted automatically. We can delete it sooner. |
| Appointments | Kept while the business exists in {{SERVICE_NAME}}, with no automatic age-based deletion of appointment history. Client details and notes are removed when the client record is erased, including under the client's separate retention period. |
| Appointment requests | 60 days after the requested appointment ends. |
| Data exports | 72 hours. |
| Legal document records | Until your account is deleted. |
| Support correspondence | {{SUPPORT_RETENTION}}. |
| Records that an account or client was erased (identifiers only) | 60 days, so that an erasure can be repeated after a backup is restored. |
| Server logs | {{LOG_RETENTION}}. |
| Internal messages between parts of our service that could not be delivered (may contain booking or profile details) | 30 days. |
| Copies of the emails we sent, kept in our sending mailbox | {{EMAIL_RETENTION}}. |
| [With Crashlytics] Crash reports | Google starts removing crash traces and associated identifiers from live and backup systems after 90 days; this is not a guaranteed completion deadline. |
| Backups | {{BACKUP_RETENTION}}. Names, contact details, notes and client descriptions in them become unreadable {{KEY_BACKUP_RETENTION}} after the person is erased ([section 8](#section-8)). |

When you delete your account in the app, the deletion takes effect 7 days later. Until then your account keeps working and you can cancel the deletion in the app; signing in does not cancel it. We notify you when you request it, 2 days and 1 day before it takes effect, and if you cancel it. If we have to keep your account for a legal reason, such as a restriction you asked for or a pending legal claim, the app tells you that it can't be deleted right now, and a deletion you have already requested waits until that reason ends. A deletion you request by email ([section 9](#section-9)) takes effect once you confirm it, without the 7-day wait.

When the deletion takes effect, we delete your profile, sign-in data, devices and notification settings, delete your client and employee records, cancel your upcoming appointments, and erase your client details and note from appointments and your employee name from the appointments you served; those appointments keep an identifier that no longer belongs to any account. Cancellation and price adjustment reasons and other appointment details remain; they must not contain personal details. Remaining appointment identifiers, service and time details, or identifying text in other fields can still allow a person to be identified. Your name, contact details and notes are encrypted with a key of your own, which the deletion destroys. Copies of that key in our backups are deleted within {{KEY_BACKUP_RETENTION}}, so after that those details can no longer be read from any backup; your other data leaves the backups as they expire. If we ever restore a backup taken before the deletion, we erase your data again before the service resumes.

If you own the business, deleting your account also deletes the business, its employees, clients, services and appointments, including when your unused account is deleted. Export the business's data and satisfy any separate legal record-keeping duties before deletion.

## <a id="section-8"></a>8. How we protect it

Sign-in uses passkeys[ or a confirmation signed by Telegram], so there is no password to steal. Connections are encrypted (TLS). Each person's name, email address, phone number,[ Telegram user ID and username,] notes and client description, and the addresses[ and chats] we send notifications to, are encrypted with a key of that person's own, which is itself encrypted with a master key kept outside the database; deleting the person destroys their key ([section 7](#section-7)). To find an account or client by email address or phone number,[ an account by Telegram user ID,] a client by the first letters of their first or last name, or appointments by a client's full name, without decrypting them, we store keyed hashes (HMAC) of those values, deleted with the record. Each employee only sees what their permissions allow. ## <a id="section-9"></a>9. Your rights

You have the right to:

- **access and export your data.** In the app, request a data export (once a day); it is kept for 72 hours. The request is confirmed with your passkey[ or, in the mini app, through a message from our Telegram bot]. If you can no longer use the app, write to us and we will send you a copy.
- **correct it.** Edit your profile in the app. Changing your email address needs a code sent to the new address.
- **delete it.** Delete your account in the app, in Settings → Account; this is confirmed with your passkey[ or, in the mini app, through a message from our Telegram bot]. The deletion takes effect 7 days later, and you can cancel it in the app until then. To have only your client record deleted, contact us. If you can no longer use the app or can't confirm the deletion in it, write to [{{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}](mailto:{{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}) from your account's email address[; if the account was created through Telegram and has no real email address, tell us and we will agree another way to confirm it is you]. We carry out a deletion requested by email once you confirm it, without the 7-day wait. What is deleted, what is kept and for how long is described in [section 7](#section-7).
- **object** to processing based on our legitimate interests, and ask us to **restrict** processing.
- **withdraw your consent** at any time, without affecting what we did before.
- **complain** to {{SUPERVISORY_AUTHORITY}}. Where GDPR applies, you may also complain to a supervisory authority in the EEA country of your habitual residence, workplace or the alleged infringement.

These rights apply under the conditions and exceptions in the applicable law; portability is distinct from the app's export feature.

For anything you cannot do in the app, write to [{{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}](mailto:{{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}). The app's daily export limit and 72-hour download window do not limit your statutory rights. We may ask for proportionate information to verify your identity, including an alternative if you cannot access your account email. Where GDPR applies, we respond without undue delay and within one month; a permitted extension of up to two further months is explained within the first month. Any shorter mandatory local deadline applies. Requests are normally free; any lawful refusal or fee is explained with the available remedies. For requests outside GDPR, the applicable response period is {{RESPONSE_TIME}}, subject to any shorter mandatory deadline.
